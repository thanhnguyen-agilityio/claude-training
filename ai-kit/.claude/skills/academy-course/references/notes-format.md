# Notes format

Match `resources/claude-academy/claude-101/`.

## README.md

```markdown
# <Course title> — study notes

Personal study notes for the Claude Academy course [<Course title>](<course URL>) (<N> lessons<, quiz, badge>).

Study guide (interactive review): [<Course title> Study Guide](<artifact URL>#overview)

These are my own summaries, not a copy of the course. Open the lesson links for the videos,
transcripts, full exercise text and the interactive exercises.

## Source and license
- Course © Anthropic. <License the page states, or "The course page text I used does not state a license.">
- These notes are personal summaries for non-commercial training use.

## The course in one page
<2–3 sentence thesis>, one summary table, "Simple rules to remember" (4–6 bullets).

## Lessons
| # | Lesson | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [<title>](<lesson URL>) | [01-<section>.md](01-<section>.md) |

<Lessons with interactives; what is not covered.>

## Diligence statement
I wrote these notes with Claude. Claude read the course pages via Claude in Chrome and drafted
summaries in its own words. I'm responsible for checking them against the course.
```

## Section file

```markdown
# Lessons <a>–<b>: <Section name>

<One line on what the section covers.>

## Lesson <n> — <Title> (<duration>)

- Short bullets; **bold** the term being defined. Tables for comparisons and steps.

**Exercise (interactive):** <one line> (only summarized)
**Reflect:** <reflection question, paraphrased>
```

15–40 lines per lesson. Quote at most a few words.
