# Day-by-Day Schedule

Week 1 · Everyone · Weeks 2–3 · Split tracks · Days 12–15 · Capstone

Three weeks plus a closing capstone block, ~6 hours of hands-on work per day plus assigned courses. Week 1 is everyone; Weeks 2–3 run the two tracks in parallel; Days 12–15 are a cross-functional capstone. Checkpoints land on Days 3, 6, 11, and 15.

Daily rhythm for the lead: 15-min morning briefing (today's goal, yesterday's gaps), hands-on blocks below, end-of-day 10-min standup where each person says what they shipped.

All work happens in the sandbox training repo(s) from the [README pre-flight](index.md) — never on client code. "Course:" lines refer to the [course catalog](courses.md). Ticking "Mark day as complete" on a day page is saved in this browser, or to your Google account when you sign in.
