# Lesson 12 — Diligence statement: email digest

## Reflection (my own words)

- **Creation:** I used Claude with my company email: the connector is linked to my company
  Gmail. Gmail access is read-only, because the skill only needs to classify mail. The digest
  goes to a Slack DM on my company Slack account, where I'm the only person. We use Slack for
  daily communication.
- **Transparency:**
  - Claude helped sharpen my idea, make the goal clear, advise on delegation, write the plan,
    run the tests, make recommendations, and write the skill.
  - I brought the idea and the goal, and gave the verdicts, decisions and corrections. I checked
    everything against real data.
- **Deployment:** I checked the real results and the dry run, and I take responsibility for
  sharing the kit.

## Statement

> In building the email-digest skill, I collaborated with Claude: on claude.ai, with the Gmail
> (read-only) and Slack connectors, to plan the project, run the search tests on my company Gmail,
> and draft SKILL.md; and in Claude Code, to review the plan and the draft and keep the project
> notes.
>
> I set the idea, goals and scope, and made every decision. I corrected Claude where it was
> wrong (the group address spelling, and claims it hadn't verified), and checked each result
> against my real mailbox, including a manual Gmail search for the dry-run window and the dry
> run posted to my own Slack DM.
>
> The skill reads company email that includes leave/WFH requests and mentee details. It
> never writes to Gmail, and it posts only to my private Slack DM. No real email content is
> stored in this repo. Before sharing the kit, I will remove my own config values (group
> addresses, project labels) so each user fills in their own.
>
> I take full responsibility for the skill, its accuracy, and how it handles company data
> before I share it with others.
