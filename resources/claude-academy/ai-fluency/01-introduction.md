# Lessons 1–3: Introduction, why AI Fluency, the 4D Framework

## Lesson 1 — Introduction to AI Fluency (4 min video)

- The course is about **collaborating** with AI, not about how AI technology works.
- It aims for skills that outlast today's tools, not quick prompt tricks.
- Definition: AI Fluency is the ability to work with AI **effectively, efficiently, ethically
  and safely**.
- The core of the course is the 4Ds: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence.
- Getting good results takes practical skills *and* a change in how you think about working
  with AI.

**Setup:** you need access to any chat model (a free Claude.ai account is enough).

**Reflect:**
- Where has AI failed to give you what you wanted?
- What kind of AI collaboration excites you?
- What do you want from this course?

## Lesson 2 — Why do we need AI Fluency?

Being "fluent" means having the skills, knowledge, insight and values to use AI well. The
lesson introduces three modes of engagement:

- **Automation**: AI runs a specific task from your instructions.
- **Augmentation**: you and AI co-think and co-create.
- **Agency**: you set up how the AI knows and behaves, so it can act on its own for you,
  instead of giving it one task at a time.

## Lesson 3 — The 4D Framework (5 min video)

| D | In short |
| --- | --- |
| Delegation | Choose what to do with AI and what to do yourself |
| Description | Communicate clearly with the AI |
| Discernment | Judge AI output and behavior critically |
| Diligence | Use AI responsibly |

- All 4Ds apply in all three modes (Automation, Augmentation, Agency).
- Practicing them now prepares you for future AI capabilities.

**Exercise — apply the 4Ds to a scenario.** Pick one and answer one question per D:

- *Communication*: drafting a marketing email series.
- *Research*: analyzing a large dataset for a paper.
- *Creative*: building characters for a story.

For each one: what you keep vs. share (Delegation), what context and success criteria the AI
needs (Description), how you check the result (Discernment), and what disclosure or
responsibility issues come up (Diligence).

**Reflect:** Which D are you strongest in, and which is weakest? Think of a recent AI chat
where the framework would have helped.
