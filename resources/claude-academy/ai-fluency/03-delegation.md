# Lessons 6–7: Delegation

## Lesson 6 — A closer look at Delegation (6 min video)

Delegation is deciding what work stays with you, what you do together with AI, and what AI
does on its own, and then splitting the work.

| Part | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Problem Awareness | Know your goal and the work it takes *before* bringing in AI |
| Platform Awareness | Know what each AI system can and cannot do |
| Task Delegation | Split the work so humans and AI each do what they're best at |

- Good delegation needs **domain expertise** plus an understanding of **AI capabilities**.
- The goal is not to automate everything. It's the best human–AI partnership for this task.

**Exercise — analyze a small task with Claude.** Pick something small (an email, a slide
outline, a meeting plan). Ask Claude to help you build a delegation plan, and actually discuss
it with Claude instead of just listing answers:
- What does a good result look like?
- Which pieces of work are needed to get there?
- Which pieces need human expertise, creativity or judgment?

Finish with a short plan that uses both sides' strengths.

## Lesson 7 — Project planning and Delegation (exercise lesson)

Starts a **course project** that you carry through the later lessons.

1. **Choose a project**: multi-step, about 1 hour of work, and something you care about.
   Possible types: communication (presentation, explainer post, pitch), research (trend
   summary, data analysis, comparison), creative (story outline, website structure, product
   concept), or learning (study plan, resource list, tutorial).
2. **Vision and goals**: let Claude ask you questions until you know what success looks like
   and why the project matters to you.
3. **Task breakdown and delegation**: list the major tasks together. For each task, ask what
   skills it needs, where human strengths matter, where AI is a good fit, and where working
   together helps most. Push back on assumptions.
4. **Save the plan**. You will use it again for Description, Discernment and Diligence.

**Reflect:** What surprised you in the planning chat? Which part of the plan looks hardest?
What would help you delegate better?
