# Lessons 4–5: Deep Dive 1 — What is generative AI?

A two-part technical lesson. It gives the background you need to delegate well.

## Lesson 4 — Generative AI fundamentals

- Generative AI **creates new content** (text, images, code). Older AI mostly classified or
  analyzed existing data.
- Large language models (LLMs) such as Claude became possible because three things came
  together:
  1. Algorithm breakthroughs, especially the **transformer** architecture
  2. Huge amounts of digital training data
  3. Much more computing power
- Two training stages:
  - **Pre-training**: learning patterns from a massive amount of text
  - **Fine-tuning**: learning to follow instructions and give helpful answers
- Other key ideas: the **context window** (how much the model can "see" at once) and
  **emergent capabilities** (skills that show up at scale without being trained directly).

## Lesson 5 — Capabilities & limitations (7 min video)

**Strengths**
- Handles many kinds of language tasks
- Keeps track of the conversation
- Switches between very different tasks without retraining
- Can connect to external tools

**Limitations**
- **Knowledge cutoff**: knows nothing after its training data ends
- **Hallucinations**: confident but false output
- **Context window limits**: long inputs can be cut off or lose detail
- **Reasoning**: still weak on some complex, multi-step problems

The field changes fast. The best results come from combining strengths: humans bring critical
thinking, judgment, creativity and ethical oversight.
