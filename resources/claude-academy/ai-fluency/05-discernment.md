# Lessons 10–11: Discernment and the Description-Discernment loop

## Lesson 10 — A closer look at Discernment

Discernment is the other side of Description. Description sends your intent to the AI;
Discernment checks whether what comes back meets your needs.

| Part | You evaluate… | Look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | the output itself | accuracy, fit, coherence, relevance |
| Process | how the AI got there | logic errors, missed points, bad reasoning |
| Performance | how the AI behaved with you | was its style useful, did it follow your feedback |

- Discernment and Description work together in an ongoing feedback loop.
- Even the strongest models still need human judgment and oversight.

**Exercise — expert Discernment.** Pick a topic you know very well. Ask Claude for three
different explanations of one aspect of it, then judge them:
- *Product*: Which is most accurate? Any errors? Is the level of detail right?
- *Process*: Is the reasoning sound? Are there gaps? Are the concepts connected well?
- *Performance*: Did it answer your actual question? Did it use the right terms? Did the tone
  help?

Tell Claude why the best one works and what's wrong with the weakest one, then improve it
together. Finally, discuss how a non-expert would have done at judging them. The point:
**domain knowledge drives good discernment.**

**Reflect:** Which type of Discernment is hardest for you? What warning signs mean an output
needs a closer look?

## Lesson 11 — The Description-Discernment loop (project exercise)

Carry out the course project from Lesson 7.

1. **Review your plan** and adjust it with what you've learned since.
2. **Agree on the Description up front** with Claude: product (outputs, format, length,
   detail), process (methods, steps) and performance (concise/detailed,
   challenging/supportive).
3. **Run the loop for each task:**
   - **Describe** the product, process and performance you want
   - **Discern** the product, the process and the AI's behavior
   - **Refine** with feedback and better descriptions, and iterate
   - **Integrate** your own expertise; you decide what stays and you own the result

**Reflect:** Which kinds of descriptions worked best? Was Description or Discernment more
work? How far did you drift from the original plan?
