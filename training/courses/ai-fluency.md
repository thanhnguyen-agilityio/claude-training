# AI Fluency: human-AI collaboration — notes

Course: [Claude Academy](https://academy.claude.com/) · Day 1 (optional), continued Day 5 · Progress: 10/15

## Lesson 1: What is AI Fluency
- AI Fluency = working with AI systems effectively, efficiently, ethically and safely.
- Framework: the **4Ds** = Delegation + Description + Discernment + Diligence.

## Lesson 2: Why it matters (3 ways people engage with AI)
- **Automation:** AI completes a specific task from your instructions.
- **Augmentation:** you and AI collaborate as partners in creative thinking and task execution.
- **Agency:** you configure AI to work independently: set up its knowledge and behavior patterns
  instead of handing it one specific task.

## Lesson 3: The 4Ds framework
- **Delegation:** decide carefully what AI does and what you do yourself.
- **Description:** communicate clearly with the AI system.
- **Discernment:** evaluate AI output and behavior with a critical eye.
- **Diligence:** make sure you use AI responsibly.

## Lesson 4: Generative AI fundamentals
- LLMs can create content. Made possible by the transformer architecture, vast datasets and
  powerful computing.
- They learn through pre-training, then fine-tuning.
- Key concepts: context window, capabilities.

## Lesson 5: Capabilities and limitations
- Capabilities: many languages, long conversations, switching between tasks.
- Limitations: knowledge cutoff, hallucination, context window size, reasoning challenges.

## Lesson 6: Delegation competency
Deciding what to do yourself, what to collaborate on with AI, and how to split tasks well.
1. **Problem awareness:** understand your goal and the work needed to reach it.
2. **Platform awareness:** know what different AI systems can do.
3. **Task delegation:** divide work between you and AI strategically.

## Lesson 8: Description competency
1. **Product description:** clearly define what you want AI to create.
2. **Process description:** guide how AI approaches the work.
3. **Performance description:** how you want AI to behave during the collaboration.

## Lesson 9: Effective prompting techniques
1. Give context.
2. Show examples of the desired output.
3. Specify constraints.
4. Break complex tasks into steps.
5. Ask AI to think first.
6. Define AI's role or tone.

## Lesson 10: Discernment competency
1. **Product discernment:** evaluate the quality of the AI output.
2. **Process discernment:** evaluate how AI approached the task.
3. **Performance discernment:** evaluate how AI behaved during the interactive session.

Note: Description and Discernment mirror each other (product / process / performance):
describe each one up front, then check each one afterwards.
