# Task 2: Clarifying questions

## Scenario
You have the five tickets and your gap list from Task 1. In a team you would role-play with a partner: one person is the reporter, the other is the developer. Working solo, Claude plays the reporter.

## How to run it
1. Pick the two tickets with the most gaps. Ticket 5 is a good first choice.
2. Write the questions you would ask the reporter. Keep each one short, with one answer possible, and say why you need it.
3. Put Claude in the reporter's role. Tell it what the reporter knows and doesn't know, for example: "You are the support lead who wrote this ticket. You know the symptom but not the cause. Answer only what a support lead would know, and say 'I don't know' otherwise."
4. Ask your questions one at a time, or all at once, then see which answers change your first step.
5. Swap roles for one ticket: let Claude write the questions and you answer as the reporter.

## Checklist
- [ ] Questions written by you first, for 2 tickets
- [ ] Claude played the reporter with limits on what it knows
- [ ] At least one answer changed your first step
- [ ] You noticed at least one case where the reporter invented a detail (a test of whether Claude respected the limits)
- [ ] The swapped round done for one ticket

## Rules of thumb for good questions
- One question, one fact. Not "Can you tell me more?"
- Ask for evidence (a log, a screenshot, a conversation id) before asking for opinions.
- Ask what the reporter expected, and what they saw.
- Ask about the environment and the time it started.
